Editor's note

Each dataset is anchored to its latest available date. The current count covers seven trailing days; the comparison is the average weekly count over the preceding 28 days. Permit unit changes are reported as proposals in a filing, not as approved or completed housing. Routine high-volume 311 categories are deliberately downweighted in headline selection so they do not crowd out business, development and other neighborhood signals. A higher record count is not inherently good or bad. Police records represent reported incidents, not a measure of victimization or neighborhood safety.
