Methodology & source limits

{{ real_estate.source_note }} Residential coverage is a published weekly transaction list rather than a complete live MLS feed. Commercial coverage is intentionally selective: an article is included only when the Bulletin can identify the property location, transaction price and a specific sale/closing date. Price per square foot is calculated only when usable square footage is published. Transactions that cannot be mapped to a DataSF parcel remain eligible for the citywide tape but do not appear under a neighborhood.
