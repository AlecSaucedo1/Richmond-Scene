How to read the verdicts

Agreement in direction is not the same as verifying a politician’s exact figure or causal explanation.

Directionally supported

The current Bulletin data moves in the same direction as the statement, with timeframe or scope caveats.

Mixed

Some data aligns and some does not, or the claim combines several ideas the data measures differently.

Current data runs the other way

The latest short-window signal moves opposite the framing. This is a flag for scrutiny, not proof the original statement was false.

Not directly testable

The claim concerns something outside the current feeds, such as fiscal forecasts, traffic speeds, causation or policy effectiveness.
