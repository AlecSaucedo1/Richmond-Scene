How to read this page

The city page aggregates neighborhood-level public-record counts and compares the latest seven-day window with the preceding four-week weekly average. It now prioritizes signals using deviation from baseline plus project scale, housing-unit changes, use changes and record volume; routine high-volume records receive less weight. News discovery combines broad city searches with targeted searches generated from the strongest neighborhood signals. Each article may anchor only one citywide crossover, which reduces repeated coverage. Matches are editorial context, not causal claims.
